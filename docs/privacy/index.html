Privacy Policy

Black Cloud Binaural (Android) · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

In short: the app works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data to us or anyone else. The only data that leaves the app is what you choose to share (an exported audio file) or choose to save to Health Connect.

Data stored on your phone

  • Presets: the settings of built-in and your own presets (name, tone and pulse frequencies, length, sound mode, background noise on/off).
  • Settings: your appearance choice and whether Health Connect logging is on.

This data stays in the app's private storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's own device backup may include it if you have backup turned on; that is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Health Connect

Logging to Health Connect is off by default. If you turn it on and grant permission, then for each listening session of one minute or more the app writes one mindfulness session record containing its start time, end time and the preset name.

  • The app only writes this data. It does not request permission to read, and never reads, any Health Connect data.
  • The data is used only to record your sessions in Health Connect. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to us or any third party. Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can turn logging off in the app's settings, revoke the permission in Health Connect, and delete the records in Health Connect at any time.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show playback controls (pause, resume, stop) while a session plays.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep audio playing with the screen off.
  • Health Connect (write mindfulness): only if you turn logging on, as described above.

The app checks which audio outputs are connected (to tell whether headphones are in use) on your phone only; this information is not stored or sent anywhere.

Exported audio

When you export a preset as a WAV file, the file is created in the app's temporary storage and handed to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. What happens next is up to that app. Exports older than an hour are deleted the next time you export.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
